# Current Project Brief - Virtual Farm Stand

> **Source of Truth:** `/SPEC.md` (root of project)

## Project Overview

**Virtual Farm Stand** is a digital roadside farm stand platform enabling farmers to list products, accept reservations, and manage waitlists—without native payments.

**Target Users:**
- Small farms, homesteads, cottage-food producers, local agricultural sellers
- Customers seeking fresh local products

## Core Value Proposition

A zero-fee platform connecting local farmers directly with customers. Farmers list what's available; customers reserve and arrange pickup/payment directly with the farmer (via external links like Venmo/PayPal).

## MVP Goals

1. ✅ Farmer registration with seller responsibility acknowledgement
2. ✅ Farm profile management (name, location, contact, payment links)
3. ✅ Product listings with availability tracking
4. ✅ Public farm pages with product browsing
5. ✅ Reservation workflow (request → confirm/decline)
6. ✅ Waitlist for unavailable products
7. ✅ Admin moderation capabilities
8. ✅ Platform disclaimers (no native payments)

## Key Constraints (Out of Scope)

- No native checkout/payment processing
- No shopping cart
- One farm per account (MVP)
- No automatic email notifications (placeholder UI only)
- No complex ad marketplace
