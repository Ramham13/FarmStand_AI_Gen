# Legal Disclaimer Drafts

> ⚠️ **These are drafts for review. Not final legal language.**

---

## 1. Farmer Seller-Responsibility Acknowledgment

**Location:** Registration / Onboarding flow

**Draft Text:**

> By creating a farm listing on Virtual Farm Stand, you acknowledge and agree that:
> 
> - You are the seller of any products you list on this platform
> - You are solely responsible for compliance with all applicable local, state, and federal laws regarding the sale of agricultural products
> - You are solely responsible for determining product availability, pricing, and accurate descriptions
> - All transactions (payment, pickup, delivery) occur directly between you and the customer
> - Virtual Farm Stand does not process payments, verify products, or guarantee any transaction

---

## 2. Customer Direct-Farmer Transaction Notice

**Location:** Footer, product pages, reservation confirmation

**Draft Text:**

> **Notice:** All purchases and reservations made through Virtual Farm Stand are direct transactions between you and the farmer. Virtual Farm Stand is a platform for connecting farmers with customers and does not process payments, hold funds, or guarantee products.
> 
> Please arrange payment and pickup directly with the farmer using the contact information provided on their farm page.

---

## 3. No Native Payments Disclaimer

**Location:** Header, footer, farm page

**Draft Text:**

> 🔒 **No Native Payments:** Virtual Farm Stand does not process payments. All transactions are conducted directly between customers and farmers through external payment methods (Venmo, PayPal, cash, etc.).

---

## 4. Platform Removal/Suspension Rights

**Location:** Terms of Service (future), Admin policies

**Draft Text:**

> Virtual Farm Stand reserves the right to:
> 
> - Remove or suspend any farm listing at our sole discretion
> - Hide individual products that violate platform policies
> - Suspend farmer accounts for policy violations
> - Refuse to host content that we determine to be inappropriate or unlawful
> 
> Farmers who are suspended may appeal by contacting support.

---

## 5. Prohibited Content Baseline

**Location:** Seller guidelines, admin moderation

**Draft Text:**

> The following are prohibited on Virtual Farm Stand:
> 
> - Illegal products or products that violate local laws
> - Misleading or false product descriptions
> - Products that pose safety risks without proper disclosure
> - Resale of products not grown/produced by the seller
> - Content that is hateful, discriminatory, or harassment
> - Spam or deceptive listings
> 
> We reserve the right to remove any listing that violates these standards or our sole discretion.
